Fortune Printer Packs

Fortunes, jokes and kind words, ready to print.

How to use a pack

  1. Press Download on a pack below (or open it in the editor first to tweak it).
  2. Open your Fortune Printer's page (fortune.local), and under Upload a .txt file choose the file you downloaded.
  3. Pick it in Print from, or leave that on "All files mixed".

Tip: if your phone is on the printer's own "FortunePrinter" WiFi it may have no internet. Download on home WiFi or mobile data, then reconnect to upload.
